Nutritional Value of Juices: Benefits, Vitamins & What You Should Know

A simple guide from Taniya Juice Corner.

Sometimes you don't feel like eating a big meal. You only want something refreshing and tasty.

Maybe it's after a lengthy lecture. Maybe you are strolling home from school in the heat of the day. Or perhaps you just want something sweet rather than yet another carbonated beverage.

A glass of fruit juice can come in.

Fruit juices are a good source of vitamins, minerals, and naturally occurring plant compounds. As one might imagine, juice is no substitute for whole fruits, but when consumed wisely, it may be a great way to add fruit to the day.

For us, at Taniya Juice Corner, a good drink must be cool, and providing nutrients is just an added bonus.

1. A Simple Source of Vitamins

The nutrients that have different fruits contribute to the glass.

For example, citrus fruits such as oranges are well known for their vitamin C content. Other fruits contain potassium, folate, and precursors of vitamin A.

That's why it's important to have some variety. Drinking different fruits rather than the same one every day can provide you with a greater variety of nutrients.

We also have a variety of options like Mosambi Juice, Pineapple Juice, Mix-Fruit Juice and more which provide you with a range of flavours to suit your different moods.

2. Fruits Bring More Than Just Sweetness

The nutritional value of fruit isn't limited to vitamins.

Fruits are a mixture of water, carbohydrates, vitamins, minerals, and naturally occurring plant compounds. They are also a good source of antioxidants and other bioactive compounds depending on the fruit.

It's this versatility that makes fruit drinks so popular, as you can enjoy the taste and color of various fruits in a single refreshing drink.

Truth be told, it is sometimes just enough to see that bright orange, yellow or green juice to make the day that little sweeter.

3. They Can Add to Your Fluid Intake

Juices are high in water, so can be included in total fluid intake.

But there is a crucial difference: water is still the primary source of hydration. Juice may be given in addition to water. Diluted 100% fruit juice and low sodium vegetable juice are also options recommended by the CDC for drinks, and water and unsweetened beverages are important choices.

If you are coming out of a lecture feeling thirsty, then a cool juice can be refreshing but be sure to bring your water bottle along.

4. Different Fruits, Different Nutritional Profiles

The best part about fruit is the numerous variety.

  • Orange and Mosambi: popular citrus varieties, rich in vitamin C.
  • Pineapple: sweet and refreshing, naturally rich in vitamin C and manganese.
  • Banana: a source of carbohydrate and potassium, used for shakes.
  • Mango: naturally sweet and rich in nutrients such as vitamin C and vitamin A precursors.
  • Mixed fruits: try mixing different fruits together to get different flavours and nutrients together.

That's the reason why there is no "best" juice. The best option might depend on your tastes and desires, preferences and the rest of your diet.

5. The "Healthy" Part Also Depends on How You Have It

Not all drinks with a "fruit drink" label are the same.

100% fruit juice is different to a sugar sweetened fruit flavoured drink. The Dietary Guidelines have a difference between 100% fruit juice and fruit drinks with added sugars.

That's why it's important to consider what's in your beverage.

When selecting juice for nutritional benefits, opt for a fresh juice that doesn't have added sugar.

Don't think that just because it's made from fruit that you can have as much of it as you want. Though juice does contain naturally occurring sugars, there are some moderation factors. The NHS advises that, for instance, fruit juice and smoothies should be restricted to a small amount (about 150 ml) a day.

Which Juice Should You Choose?

Sometimes it's not so much about the nutrition charts and more about what you're feeling.

  • Want something citrusy? Add Mosambi juice.
  • Want something tropical? Pineapple or Mango could be your pick.
  • Want something creamy? Go for a Banana or Mango Shake.
  • Want a combination of flavours? Mix-Fruit Juice is here to help!
  • Need some coffee fix? Always Cold Coffee.

There's no one-size-fits-all beverage. Occasionally the best option is the one that you will enjoy.

When Is a Good Time to Have Juice?

There's no perfect time to drink juice. You can have it with a meal or as a treat during the day. For students it might feel especially good during a break between classes or after being outside. The key is how much you drink and what you eat overall. When you choose juice keep in mind that it's not the same as eating a fruit. Most of the fibre gets removed when you juice. That's why whole fruit should still be part of your meals.

Juice vs. Soft Drinks: What's the Difference?

When you feel thirsty it's simple to take whatever is close by. Not every drink offers the same benefits in terms of nutrition.

Regular soda drinks have extra sugars but don't add much in the way of good nutrients. A juice that is 100% from fruit can give you vitamins and other good things that are already inside the fruit.

This does not mean that juice should take the place of water or that you can have as much as you want. It just means that when you are picking a drink it is important to understand what you are actually consuming.

For water needs, water is the easiest option. Juice can be enjoyed for its taste and for the things it brings to your body as part of a healthy way of eating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is fresh juice healthy?

Fresh juice can give vitamins and minerals. It should be taken in moderation. Whole fruits usually give fibre so fresh juice should not replace them completely.

Is juice better than soft drinks?

A 100% fruit juice can give nutrients that are naturally in fruit while many soft drinks mainly give added sugars. Still, water is the everyday drink for staying hydrated.

Can I drink juice every day?

Juice can be part of a diet but the portion size matters because fresh juice can have a lot of natural sugar and usually has less fibre than whole fruit.

Which juice has the most vitamin C?

Citrus juices such as orange juice are well known for vitamin C. The exact amount depends on the fruit, the serving size and how it is prepared.

Is fruit better than juice?

Whole fruit usually has an advantage because it contains fibre and takes longer to eat. Fresh juice can still be enjoyed as one part of a diet.

Does juice replace water?

No. Fresh juice can add to intake but water should still be the main everyday source of hydration.

5 Refreshing Drinks to Beat the Summer Heat

What to sip when the sun gets a little too real.

Summer has its own way of making us feel tired, thirsty, and low on energy. Whether you're coming out of a long lecture, walking around campus, or simply looking for something refreshing, a chilled drink can instantly make the day feel a little better.

But with so many options available, what should you actually choose?

From fresh fruit juices to creamy shakes and cold coffee, here are 5 refreshing drinks that can be perfect for those hot summer days.

1. Orange Juice: A Classic Refreshment

When it comes to refreshing drinks, orange juice is hard to go wrong with.

Orange juice has a naturally sweet and slightly tangy flavour, making it a popular choice during hot weather. Oranges are also known for their vitamin C content, along with other nutrients and plant compounds.

A chilled glass of orange juice can be a simple way to add some fruit to your day while enjoying something refreshing.

Best for: Those who like something light, fruity and refreshing.
Try it when: You want something citrusy after a long class or a tiring day on campus.

2. Pineapple Juice: Sweet, Tangy & Tropical

Want something that instantly gives you summer vibes? Pineapple juice is a great choice.

Pineapple has a naturally sweet-and-tangy taste that makes it particularly refreshing when served chilled. It also provides nutrients such as vitamin C and manganese.

Its tropical flavour makes pineapple juice a fun alternative when you're bored of your regular drink.

Best for: People who enjoy a sweet and tangy flavour.
Try it when: You're looking for something fruity and refreshing on a hot afternoon.

3. Mixed Fruit Juice: A Little Bit of Everything

Can't decide which fruit you want? Why choose just one?

Mixed fruit juice combines the flavours of different fruits, creating a more interesting taste and a little more variety in every glass.

Depending on the fruits used, mixed fruit juice can provide different vitamins, minerals and plant compounds. It's also a great option for people who enjoy a combination of sweet and tangy flavours.

Best for: Those who can't pick just one flavour.
Try it when: You want something colourful, fruity and full of flavour.

4. Mango Shake: Summer's Favourite

Can we really talk about summer without mentioning mangoes?

A mango shake combines the rich, naturally sweet flavour of mango with a creamy texture, making it a more indulgent option compared with a regular fruit juice.

Mangoes provide nutrients such as vitamin C and vitamin A, while the milk used in a shake can add protein and calcium.

Best for: Anyone who wants something creamy, filling and sweet.
Try it when: You're craving a proper summer treat instead of a light drink.

5. Cold Coffee: For the Caffeine Lovers

Not everyone wants fruit juice when it's hot. Sometimes, you just need your caffeine fix.

That's where cold coffee comes in.

Chilled, creamy and energising, cold coffee can be a great option when you're looking for something different from fruit-based drinks. It can be especially popular among students who want to take a quick break between classes.

Just remember that cold coffee can contain added sugar and calories depending on how it's prepared, so it's best enjoyed in moderation.

Best for: The coffee lovers who don't want to give up their caffeine, even in summer.
Try it when: You need a refreshing break between classes or simply want something cold and creamy.

It really depends on what you're craving.

And if you're still confused... try something different each time! 😌

A Quick Reminder About Staying Hydrated 💧

While refreshing drinks can be enjoyable during summer, water should still be your main source of hydration.

Fresh juices can contribute fluids and nutrients to your diet, but drinking large quantities of juice isn't necessarily better. Fruit juice can contain naturally occurring sugars, and some prepared drinks may also contain added sugar.

A good summer routine is simple: drink enough water, eat fruits and vegetables, and enjoy refreshing drinks in moderation.

Make Your Summer Break More Refreshing ☀️

Summer doesn't have to be all about surviving the heat. Sometimes, a quick break, good company and a chilled drink are all you need to make the day a little better.

Whether you're an orange juice person, a mango shake lover, a mixed-fruit fan or someone who can't function without cold coffee, there's always a drink for the mood you're in.

So, the next time you're around campus and the summer heat starts getting a little too real, take a refreshing break and find your favourite.

Because sometimes, all you need is a little chill in the middle of a hot day. 🍹

Frequently Asked Questions

Which drinks are best for summer?

Orange juice, pineapple juice, mixed fruit juice, mango shakes and cold coffee are popular choices during summer. The best option depends on your taste and whether you prefer something fruity, tangy or creamy.

Which juice is best for staying hydrated?

Water is the best choice for everyday hydration. Fruit juices can also contribute to fluid intake, but they may contain considerable amounts of natural sugar.

Is orange juice better than pineapple juice?

Neither is universally better. Orange juice is well known for its vitamin C content, while pineapple juice offers a sweet, tangy flavour and nutrients such as vitamin C and manganese. Your choice can depend on your taste and dietary preferences.

Is cold coffee a good summer drink?

Cold coffee is a popular chilled option for coffee lovers. However, its caffeine and sugar content can vary, so it's a good idea to enjoy it in moderation.

Which refreshing drink should college students choose?

It depends on the mood! Fruit juices are great for those craving fruity flavours, mango shakes suit creamy cravings, and cold coffee is popular among caffeine lovers. Remember to drink water regularly throughout the day, too.

What is the best time to enjoy a refreshing drink in summer?

You can enjoy a refreshing drink during a study break, after a walk, or whenever you feel like having one. Make sure you stay hydrated throughout the day rather than relying only on juices or other beverages.
