About us

A regular stop at Amity's gate

Right opposite Amity University Punjab, Taniya Juice Corner has become the go-to break between classes.

Our story

We are a small juice stall with a simple idea: serve real fruit, made in front of you. Fresh juice, thick shakes and cold coffee, no shortcuts and nothing poured from a packet.

Students, staff and neighbours stop by for a quick glass between lectures, and many keep coming back for their favourite order.

What we stand for

100% fresh

Fruit squeezed to order, not poured from a packet.

Priced for students

Great taste at pocket-friendly prices, right at the university gate.

Made your way

Less sugar, no ice, extra fruit. Just ask.
